FAQ

Credentialing software questions, answered

What is provider credentialing software?

Provider credentialing software manages the process of getting healthcare providers credentialed and enrolled with payers: collecting provider data, running credentialing and payer enrollment workflows, keeping CAQH profiles current, and monitoring licenses and sanctions. CredFlow is medical credentialing software whose AI agents complete the work itself — payer portal applications, status calls, and follow-up — instead of only tracking it.

What does CredFlow automate?

CredFlow’s AI agents fill and submit payer web-portal applications, call payers to check application status and verify effective dates, keep CAQH data in sync, and run follow-up cadences — with evidence logged for every action, including screenshots, recordings, and transcripts — all inside one credentialing and enrollment software platform.

How is CredFlow different from traditional credentialing software?

Traditional credentialing software tracks tasks: it tells your team what to key into payer portals and who to call. CredFlow’s AI agents do that work — filling and submitting portal applications, calling payers for status, syncing CAQH — and log screenshots, recordings, and transcripts as evidence for every action.

Can CredFlow manage our credentialing workflows?

Yes. The credentialing workflow engine ships reusable templates for onboarding, payer enrollment, and group contracting, with owners, steps, and reminders — so the process runs the same way for every provider, every time.

Does CredFlow replace my credentialing team?

No. CredFlow is built for teams that keep credentialing in-house. Your team keeps ownership and control; the AI agents remove the manual keying, portal work, and hold time.

Can CredFlow work alongside our existing system?

Yes. Run CredFlow as your end-to-end credentialing platform and system of record, or connect it by API as a plug-in layer to the system you already have — including Salesforce-connected environments. Provider, group, and enrollment data flows in; statuses, outcomes, and evidence flow back.

How does implementation work?

Two paths: adopt CredFlow as your system of record, or start as a plug-in layer connected by API to your existing stack. Provider and enrollment data flows in, statuses and evidence flow back, and your team keeps working while the agents pick up the queue.

Does CredFlow do primary source verification?

CredFlow provides PSV workflow and data-model support today — verification checklists by provider type, source tracking, and audit-ready evidence stored by provider and date range — and integrates partner verification sources via API. Native PSV coverage continues to expand; for organizations that need a specific verification source, we connect it rather than replace it.
