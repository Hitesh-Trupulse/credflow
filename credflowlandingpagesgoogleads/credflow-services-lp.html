FAQ

Credentialing and enrollment questions, answered

What do medical credentialing services include?

CredFlow’s credentialing services cover documentation collection, specialist-reviewed applications, CAQH credentialing services (profile management, maintenance, and attestation), payer submissions, follow-up with payers, recredentialing and expirables tracking, and appeals and rejection management. The same desk provides physician and doctor credentialing services for groups of any size.

How long does payer enrollment take?

CredFlow submits complete applications within 2 business days. Payer approval timelines vary by payer, and our team follows up with each payer until the effective date is confirmed.

How long does medical credentialing take?

Credentialing timelines vary by payer and state, and no vendor controls a payer’s review queue. What CredFlow controls: applications that go out complete and specialist-reviewed, documented follow-up with every payer, and live tracking so you always know exactly where each application sits.

Which payers and states do you cover?

CredFlow handles payer enrollment across 300+ payers in all 50 states, including Medicare, Medicaid, and commercial payers. CAQH enrollment services and physician enrollment services for individual providers are included.

Do you handle provider enrollment and insurance panel enrollment?

Yes. Provider enrollment and payer enrollment are the same managed motion at CredFlow: specialists prepare and submit applications, handle insurance panel enrollment with commercial plans, and follow up until effective dates are confirmed — for individual providers and for groups.

Do we lose visibility when we outsource credentialing?

No. Every submission, status update, and payer communication is tracked live in the CredFlow platform, so your team keeps full visibility without doing the work.

Can you fix an enrollment backlog?

Yes. CredFlow takes over enrollment backlogs and revalidation cleanup, works each stalled application, and follows up with payers until effective dates are confirmed.

How is CredFlow different from other credentialing companies?

Most credentialing companies, credentialing agencies, and payer enrollment companies work out of spreadsheets and email. CredFlow is a medical credentialing company built on its own platform — every submission, payer touch, and status change is visible to your team, live.
